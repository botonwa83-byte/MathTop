## 中文

本 App 为单机数学学习工具，全部题目与解析内置在安装包中，无需注册、无需登录、无服务器交互。

- 主要功能：88 个知识点的题组练习（每个知识点 10 道）、错题变身器、能力地图、成长摘要、收藏，以及三批仿真题（100 道）集中训练。
- 免费范围：每个知识点前 3 题试练、仿真第 1 批（34 题）、错题变身器、成长摘要、收藏、能力地图浏览。
- 付费内容：完整版解锁（非消耗型内购，产品 ID `com.mathtop.app.full_unlock`，¥22 一次性买断），解锁后开放每个知识点完整 10 道题组、仿真第 2/3 批、进阶模块五个方向（竞赛压轴、几何实验室、应用题冲刺、复盘、阶段测评）。
- 内购测试路径：安装后进入「我的」页 →「解锁完整版」，可看到付费墙；也可在练习完成一个批次后从结算页解锁。沙盒账号可直接购买或点击「恢复购买」。
- 无第三方 SDK、无广告、无分析、无用户数据上传。

## English

MathTop is an offline math learning app. All questions and explanations are bundled in the app; there is no sign-up, no login and no server communication.

- Features: 88 knowledge points with 10-question sets each, mistake rework tool, ability map, growth summary, bookmarks, and three simulation batches (100 questions).
- Free tier: the first 3 questions of every knowledge point, simulation batch 1 (34 questions), mistake rework, growth summary, bookmarks and ability map are free.
- Paid tier: one-time non-consumable unlock (product ID `com.mathtop.app.full_unlock`, CNY 22) that opens the full question sets, simulation batches 2-3 and the five advanced modules.
- IAP test path: open the app → "我的" tab → "解锁完整版" to see the paywall; the end-of-set summary screen also offers unlock. Use a sandbox account to purchase, or tap "恢复购买".
- No third-party SDKs, no ads, no analytics, no data uploaded.
